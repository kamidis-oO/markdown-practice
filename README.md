# Learning Finnish: My Journey with a Difficult Language

## Why I Started

Finnish looked **impossible** at first. All those double letters and long words scared me, but I decided to try anyway. The first week I wanted to give up, and then something clicked: *small steps really work*.

### A Small Victory

After a few weeks I could order coffee in Finnish, and that felt *amazing*. The moment I understood a whole sentence without translating it in my head was ***truly unforgettable***.

## How I Study Every Day

- Short daily sessions instead of long weekend marathons
- Listening to Finnish music while commuting
- Writing new words in a small notebook
- Speaking out loud even when I am alone

## My Weekly Routine

1. Review the vocabulary from the previous week
2. Read one short news article in Finnish
3. Watch a Finnish video with subtitles
4. Practise speaking with a language partner
5. Write a short text about my day

## Helpful Resources

I mostly use [Uusi kielemme](https://uusikielemme.fi/) for grammar explanations, and the [Markdown Guide](https://www.markdownguide.org/cheat-sheet/) for writing these notes.

## A Tiny Practice Script

I keep my word list in a text file and count it with a small script:

```python
words = ["talo", "kissa", "kirja", "vesi"]
print("I know", len(words), "words so far")
```

Inline code is also handy, for example `print("hei")` or the command `git add .`.
